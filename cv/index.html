Professional Summary

Tenure-track Associate Professor at Dongguan University of Technology working on resource-efficient intelligent wireless systems, including ambient IoT, semantic communications, low-altitude intelligent networking, and wireless AI. Principal investigator of projects supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China and the Guangdong Basic and Applied Basic Research Foundation.
