About Me
======
I joined the School of Electrical Engineering and Intelligentization at Dongguan University of Technology as an Associate Professor in December 2024. I received my Ph.D. degree from Macau University of Science and Technology in 2024, where I was fortunate to be supervised by [Prof. Dong Li](https://sites.google.com/view/eedongli). I obtained a joint M.S. degree from Iowa State University in 2020, where I had the opportunity to work with [Prof. Zhengdao Wang](https://mason.gmu.edu/~zwang52/index.html), and earned my M.S. and B.S. degrees from Tatung University in 2020 and 2017, respectively. Before joining DGUT, I worked as a postdoctoral fellow at Macau University of Science and Technology.
